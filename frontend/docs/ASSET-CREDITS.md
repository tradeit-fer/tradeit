## Fonts

DM Sans and Manrope are bundled with their original Open Font License notices in `assets/fonts/licenses/`. The readable weight-specific filenames are mapped in `styles/fonts.css`.

- DM Sans source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans
- Manrope source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope

## Icons and illustration

The TradeIt mark, calculator illustration and inline interface icon paths are inherited from the original project. `assets/icons/favicon.svg` is the application icon; `assets/images/calculator.svg` is the catalogue illustration; `scripts/components/icons.js` contains the interface icon paths.
